## 📌 Problem Statement

Long-distance travelers, bikers, truck drivers, and cab drivers often face:

- Unsafe road conditions 🚧
- Driver fatigue during long journeys 😴
- Vehicle breakdown stress 🔧
- Poor access to mechanics, shelters, and rest stops 🏨
- Lack of offline emergency support 📡
- No real-time local road intelligence 🛣️

Traditional navigation apps only provide directions — they do not solve real journey problems.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

We built a **Long-Haul Travel & Navigation Assistant** that provides:

✅ Smart route optimization  
✅ Real-time 10KM local area scanning  
✅ Emergency SOS support  
✅ Vehicle-aware fuel/EV planning  
✅ Community-powered road hazard reporting  
✅ Offline-first travel safety support

This app is designed especially for:

- Truck Drivers
- Cab Drivers
- Bikers
- Long-Distance Travelers
- Students & Adventure Travelers

---

## 🔥 Key Features

---

### 🛣️ Smart Journey Segmentation

Breaks long routes like:

**Varanasi → Delhi**

into smaller safe travel laps based on:

- Weather conditions
- Driver fatigue prediction
- Road conditions
- Best driving hours

Also suggests:

- Rest stops
- Safe shelters
- Nearby food points

---

### 📍 10KM Radius Scanner

Scans the surrounding 10KM area and detects:

- Road defects
- Potholes
- Poor lighting
- Weather risks
- Accidents
- Unsafe zones

Helps users avoid dangerous areas before reaching them.

---

### 🚨 One-Tap SOS Beacon

A floating emergency panic button that:

- Sends live GPS coordinates
- Works even with low/no internet
- Alerts saved emergency contacts
- Can notify local authorities via SMS

Critical for highways and remote travel zones.

---

### ⛽ Dynamic Fuel / EV Routing

Based on:

- Vehicle type (Petrol / Diesel / EV)
- Tank capacity
- Battery percentage

The app finds:

- Cheapest fuel stations
- EV charging points
- Most cost-effective stops

along the exact travel route.

---

### 🏍️ Convoy Sync (For Bikers)

Group riding feature where bikers can:

- Create a digital room
- Share live locations
- Track each other in real time
- Get alerts if someone falls behind

Perfect for long rides and tours.

---

### 🏆 Highway Hero Rewards

Users report:

- Potholes
- Accidents
- Road closures
- Hidden scenic spots
- Safe rest areas

and earn reward points redeemable at:

- Dhabas
- Motels
- Coffee shops
- Travel partners

Community + Gamification together.
